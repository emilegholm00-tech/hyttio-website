Three ways to split

ModelWorks whenDownside
Split everything equallyYou own and use the house about equallyFeels unfair if one family is there all summer
Everything by ownership shareOwnership shares differIgnores who actually uses the house
Fixed by share, usage by useMost families – the fairest optionYou need to keep track of days or meters

Fixed costs

  • property tax
  • insurance
  • standing charges for electricity, water and waste
  • road, owners’ association and chimney sweeping
  • mortgage interest and repayments
  • a regular maintenance fund for the roof, painting and decking

Usage

  1. By number of days in the house – simple and often good enough.
  2. By meter readings: read electricity and water on arrival and departure. Hyttio has a meters tab that shows usage.
  3. Everyone pays their own: for example, you buy firewood and top up after yourself.

Avoid the usual arguments

  • Keep every receipt in one place that everyone can see.
  • Settle up regularly, for example every quarter or after the summer.
  • Agree a spending limit for what can be bought without asking.
  • Have a joint account for the fixed costs.

Let the app do the maths

In Hyttio everyone adds their own costs, ideally with a photo of the receipt. The app always shows who owes whom and suggests the fewest transfers to settle up. Costs can be split equally between everyone or just some of you, in euro or Danish, Swedish and Norwegian kroner.
