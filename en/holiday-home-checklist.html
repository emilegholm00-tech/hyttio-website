Holiday home checklist

What needs doing when you arrive, when you leave – and when the house is closed for winter? Print the list and stick it on the fridge.

Updated 12 November 2026

On arrival

  • Get the key and unlock – put it back in the key box
  • Switch on the main power and heating
  • Turn on the water and let it run for a few minutes
  • Switch on the water heater
  • Air all the rooms
  • Check for damp, mice and leaks – add them as shared chores
  • Read the electricity and water meters
  • Check the shopping list: coffee, toilet paper, washing-up liquid

On departure

  • Empty the fridge and take or sort the rubbish
  • Wash up, wipe down, vacuum and mop the floors
  • Strip the beds and wash towels (or take them home)
  • Add anything that ran out to the shopping list
  • Read the electricity and water meters
  • Add costs, e.g. firewood and gas
  • Switch off lights, oven and coffee machine – turn down the heating
  • Close windows, lock doors and shed, put the key back
  • Bring in garden furniture and cushions

Winterising

If the house is unheated in winter, burst pipes are the most expensive mistake. Check your insurance too – some policies require the water to be off and the pipes drained.

  • Turn off the main water valve and drain the pipes
  • Drain and switch off the water heater
  • Add antifreeze to traps and toilet, or empty them
  • Leave the fridge and freezer empty with the doors ajar
  • Set heating to frost protection if you have electric heaters or a heat pump
  • Clear the gutters and remove leaves from the roof
  • Remove food that might attract mice
  • Check the chimney and stove, and book a sweep
  • Let a neighbour know the house is empty

Opening in spring

  • Walk around the house: roof, windows, deck and trees
  • Turn the water on slowly and check for leaks
  • Test smoke alarms and change the batteries
  • Share out the season’s jobs: lawn, hedge, painting, oiling the deck
