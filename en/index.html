Everything in one place

  • Shared booking calendar – book arrival and departure in two taps, one colour per family
  • Costs – see who owes whom, in euro and Danish, Swedish and Norwegian kroner
  • Chores – with an owner, due date and repeats – e.g. “Clear the gutters” every autumn
  • Shopping list – so you never run out of coffee or toilet paper
  • Electricity and water – log meter readings and see usage
  • House guide – key box, wifi, fuse box and rubbish – with photos

Five things to agree on when you share a holiday home

  1. Peak season: Take turns choosing weeks first, or have fixed weeks that rotate each year.
  2. Fixed costs: Property tax, insurance and standing charges are often split by ownership share.
  3. Usage: Electricity, firewood and water can be split by nights stayed or by meter readings.
  4. Upkeep: Who clears the gutters, paints and mows? Keep a shared list of chores.
  5. If someone wants out: Agree on a right of first refusal and how the price is set. Put it in writing, ideally with a lawyer.

Questions and support

What does Hyttio cost?

Hyttio is free for everyone in the house. No ads and no account.

Does everyone need an iPhone?

Yes, Hyttio is for iPhone and everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud. Bookings can also be shown in Google Calendar and Outlook.

How do I invite the others?

Go to the House tab and tap “Create invitation link”. Send it by text, Messenger or email.

Where is our data stored?

Everything is stored in iCloud with Apple and shared only with the people you invite. We can’t see your data.
